One quick fix first

Windows needs a driver to talk to your portal. A free tool called Zadig adds it. You only do this once per portal.
  1. Download Zadig and open it.
  2. Pick your portal in the list. If it isn't there, click Options, then List All Devices.
  3. Check the box after the green arrow says WinUSB, then click Install Driver (or Replace Driver).
